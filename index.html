🌐 Being Safe Online


Welcome to this Learning Experience!
 

In this project, you are going to learn how to be SAFE and RESPONSIBLE on the internet. You will use English to talk about dangers online and what to do to protect yourself and your friends.
 

What are we going to do?
•       Watch videos and read texts about internet safety.

•       Learn key vocabulary in English.

•       Play games and do activities to practise what you learn.

•       Create an interactive INFOGRAPHIC about how to be safe online.

•       Film a short VIDEO showing dangerous situations and how to react.

 

How long?
We will work on this project for 6 sessions (about 45–60 minutes each).

 

What do you need?
•       A device with internet access (tablet, computer, or mobile phone).

•       Your creativity and your English!

 

♿  ACCESSIBILITY NOTE (DUA): All videos have subtitles. All images have descriptions. If you need support in Spanish at any point, ask your teacher.
 

Your final products:

Topic: "How to be safe online"

Tool: Canva or Genially

Must include: clickable elements!

🎬 PRODUCT 2

Short Video (role-play)

Topic: dangerous situations & solutions

Tool: Clipchamp, iMovie or phone

Length: 1–2 minutes
