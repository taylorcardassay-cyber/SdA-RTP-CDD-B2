Session 6 - Share and Reflect

Overview

🎯 Session goals
•       Share your infographic and video with the class.

•       Give and receive feedback in English.

•       Reflect on what you have learned.

 

♿  DUA: You can present sitting down or from your seat. If you feel nervous speaking in public, you may read from your notes. Your teacher will give you a printed copy of the rubric if you need one.
 

🎤 Presentation time

Each group will:

1.     Show their interactive infographic on the screen (projected via Teams or browser).

2.     Explain 3 of their internet safety tips in English.

3.     Play their video.

4.     Answer one question from the class.

💬 Giving feedback — Sentence starters

Positive feedback ✅ Suggestions 💡

«I really liked...»

«Your infographic was very clear because...»

«I learnt something new: ...»

«Maybe next time you could...»

«One idea to improve it: ...»

«I think it would be better if...»

📝 Self-assessment — Teams

SELF-ASSESSMENT TASK (Teams form): Your teacher will share a form on Teams. Answer these questions honestly:  

1. What did I do well in this project?

2. What was the most difficult part?

3. What do I know now about internet safety that I didn't know before?

4. I would like to improve...

5. My effort in this project: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (choose 1–5)  

Complete this before leaving the class.
