Session 4 - Create your infographic

Overview

🎯 Session goals

•       Design an interactive infographic in English.

•       Summarise the main safety tips visually.

•       Practise teamwork and digital creativity.

 

♿  DUA: You can choose the tool you feel most comfortable with: Canva or Genially. You may also work with a partner if you prefer. If English is difficult, write your ideas in Spanish first, then translate them together.

📚 What is an infographic?
An infographic uses images, icons and short texts to explain information in a visual and clear way. Your infographic will be INTERACTIVE — that means it will have clickable elements!

 

📋 Instructions


1.     Open Canva (canva.com) or Genially (genially.com) with your school email.

2.     Choose the template your teacher has shared (see link on Teams).

3.     Add at least 5 internet safety tips in English.

4.     Include at least 3 images or icons.

5.     Add at least 1 interactive element (clickable link, button, animation or tooltip).

6.     Include a title: «How to be safe online».

7.     Write the names of your group members.

8.     When finished: share the link with your teacher on Teams.

💡 Useful language for your infographic

Giving advice Warnings Imperatives

You should...

It's important to...

Make sure you...

Be careful with...

Watch out for...

Never + verb...

Don't share...

Protect your...

Tell an adult...

📤 How to submit it

When your infographic is ready:

1. In Canva: click «Share» > «Copy link». Paste the link in the Teams assignment.

2. In Genially: click «Share» > «Publish» > enable the public link. Copy and paste it.  Your teacher will check that the interactive elements work correctly.
