Glossary

📖 Key vocabulary — Being Safe Online

TERM DEFINITION (English) EN ESPAÑOL
Cyberbullying Using technology to hurt, threaten or embarrass someone repeatedly. Ciberacoso
Password A secret word or code used to access an account or device. Contraseña
Phishing A trick to steal personal information by pretending to be a trustworthy source. Suplantación de identidad / phishing
Grooming When an adult builds a relationship with a child online to harm them. Grooming (acoso sexual a menores)
Personal information Details that identify a person: name, address, phone number, photo, school. Información personal
Privacy The right to keep your personal information safe and to yourself. Privacidad
Stranger A person you don't know in real life. Desconocido/a
Screen time The amount of time spent using a screen (phone, tablet, computer, TV). Tiempo de pantalla
Trusted adult A grown-up you trust and can talk to if something worries you (parent, teacher). Adulto/a de confianza
Block To stop someone from contacting you online. Bloquear
Report To tell an adult or a platform that something or someone is not safe. Denunciar / reportar
Download To copy a file from the internet to your device. Descargar
Https:// The beginning of a secure website address. The 's' means 'secure'. Https:// (protocolo seguro)
Social media Online platforms where people share content and communicate (Instagram, TikTok...). Redes sociales
Virus A harmful programme that can damage your device or steal information. Virus informático
