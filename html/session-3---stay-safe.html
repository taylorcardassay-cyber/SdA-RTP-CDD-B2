Session 3 - Stay safe!

Overview

🎯 Session goals
•       Learn rules and tips to be safe online.

•       Practise giving advice using can / can't and imperatives.

•       Create a personal digital safety decalogue in English.

 

♿  DUA: You can listen to the texts using the text-to-speech tool in your browser. If you prefer, you can write your decalogue in Spanish and then translate it with the support of your teacher.

📚 Read: The 10 Golden Rules for Internet Safety

1. Never share your full name, address, phone number or school online.
2. Use a strong password. Mix letters, numbers and symbols. Never share it.
3. Don't talk to strangers online. Only accept contact from people you know in real life.
4. Think before you post. Anything you share online can stay there forever.
5. Don't open suspicious links, files or emails from people you don't know.
6. Tell a trusted adult (parent, teacher) if something online makes you feel uncomfortable or scared.
7. Set screen time limits. Rest your eyes and take breaks every hour.
8. Check if a website is safe (look for 'https://' and the padlock icon).
9. Be kind online. Treat others the way you want to be treated.
10. If someone is unkind to you online: don't reply, block them, and tell an adult.


Fill in the blanks

Complete the golden rules with the correct word. Word bank: password · adult · kind · strangers · post
