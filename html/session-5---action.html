Being safe online

SdA

Session 5 - Action!

Overview

🎯 Session goals

•       Plan and film a short role-play video in English.

•       Show a dangerous situation online and the correct solution.

•       Practise spoken English in an authentic context.

 

♿  DUA: You can choose your role in the group: actor, director, script writer or editor. All roles are equally important. Subtitles are recommended but not obligatory.

🎬 Your video — What should it include?

•       A short situation where someone is in danger online (e.g., receiving a message from a stranger, seeing cyberbullying).

•       A response: what the person does to stay safe.

•       At least 3 characters.

•       All dialogue in English (it is OK to speak slowly — clarity is more important than speed!).

•       Duration: 1–2 minutes.

Reading Activity

Fill in the gaps with the following words.

Word bank: tell · block · stranger · uncomfortable · password

📋 Script planner — Teams

📝 BEFORE FILMING:

Open Teams > «Being Safe Online» > «Script Planning» tab.

With your group, decide:    

• What is the dangerous situation?    

• Who are the characters?    

• What does each person say?    

• How does it end safely?  

Write your script (minimum 8 lines of dialogue). Your teacher must approve it before you start filming.

🎥 How to film and edit

 
1.     Film with a tablet, phone or webcam.

2.     Use Clipchamp (Windows) or iMovie (Apple) to edit.

3.     Add a title at the beginning: «Being Safe Online — [Your Group Name]».

4.     Optional: add subtitles.

5.     Export as MP4.

6.     Upload to Teams: assignment «Session 5 — Our Video».

💡 Useful phrases for your video

•       «I got a message from a stranger online...»

•       «Don't share your personal information!»

•       «We should tell a teacher / our parents.»

•       «That doesn't feel safe. Let's block them.»

•       «If something feels wrong online, always speak up!»
