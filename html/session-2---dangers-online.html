📚 Read and learn: Main dangers online

⚠️ CYBERBULLYING

Cyberbullying means hurting, threatening or making someone feel bad online. It can happen by text, video, photo or social media.

What to do: Don't reply. Block the person. Tell a trusted adult.

🎣 PHISHING

Phishing is when someone pretends to be a real company or person to steal your personal information, like passwords or bank details.

What to do: Don't click suspicious links. Check the email address. Ask an adult.

👤 GROOMING

Grooming is when an adult contacts a child online to gain their trust and then try to harm them. It can happen in games, chats or social media.

What to do: Never meet online contacts in real life. Tell a trusted adult immediately.

📵 ADDICTION

Spending too much time online can affect your sleep, school work and relationships. Setting screen time limits is important.

What to do: Set a timer. Take breaks. Do outdoor activities every day.
